# Manufacturer Partnerships & Product Distribution Strategy

## Purpose

ROBOSCOPIA is intended to grow from an independent robotics and CNC knowledge platform into a trusted discovery and commercial connection channel between manufacturers, authorized distributors, system integrators and equipment buyers.

This is a strategic direction, not a claim that ROBOSCOPIA currently has manufacturer agreements, distributor status, inventory, pricing feeds or authority to sell any manufacturer's products.

## Strategic principle

**Technical independence and commercial cooperation must coexist.**

- Manufacturer-controlled websites, manuals, datasheets and product catalogs are preferred primary sources for the manufacturer's own product facts.
- Manufacturer information must be attributed to its source and tied to the exact model, generation, configuration and document revision where possible.
- Manufacturer status as a primary source does not automatically verify every extracted claim; the relevant document and model/configuration still need review.
- Commercial payments, sponsorships, affiliate commissions or referral agreements must never change technical facts, evidence status, ranking methodology or objective comparisons.
- Sponsored placements and commercial links must be clearly disclosed.
- ROBOSCOPIA must not imply that it is an official partner, authorized reseller or distributor without a current written agreement that grants that status.

## Partnership progression

### Stage 1 — Build a credible product knowledge platform

- Publish accurate, source-attributed manufacturer and product profiles.
- Organize models, generations, variants, technical specifications, applications, documentation and media rights.
- Link users to official manufacturer product pages and authorized sales/service channels.
- Provide transparent comparisons based on equivalent configurations and traceable evidence.
- Offer manufacturers a clear process to submit corrections, updated documents and structured product information.

### Stage 2 — Manufacturer visibility and qualified enquiries

After the platform has useful coverage and relevant audience traffic, offer opt-in manufacturer profiles and product discovery opportunities.

Potential services:
- Enhanced manufacturer and product pages.
- Clearly labeled sponsored product presentations.
- Qualified buyer enquiry forms (RFQs).
- Requests for quotations, demonstrations, technical consultations or local distributor contact.
- Referral tracking and reporting, subject to consent and applicable privacy rules.

A lead must contain only information the user knowingly submits for the stated purpose. Personal data must not be shared with a manufacturer or distributor without an appropriate legal basis and clear user notice/consent where required.

### Stage 3 — Commercial referrals and sales partnerships

Potential models, each subject to a separate agreement:
- Referral fees for qualified enquiries.
- Affiliate commissions for attributable purchases.
- Pay-per-lead arrangements with transparent qualification criteria.
- Manufacturer or distributor campaigns with clear sponsorship disclosure.
- Product catalog, inventory or pricing-feed integrations under an approved license/API agreement.

### Stage 4 — Authorized distribution or regional representation

Longer-term possibility:
- Become an officially appointed sales or distribution partner for selected manufacturers and territories.
- Negotiate territory, product scope, sales targets, training, warranty/service responsibilities, spare-parts access, pricing rules and after-sales obligations.
- Only advertise an authorized distributor/reseller status after the agreement is signed, valid and covers the relevant products and territory.

ROBOSCOPIA must not take orders, collect deposits, promise delivery, offer warranties or act as seller of record unless the legal, operational, payment, tax, consumer-protection and after-sales requirements have been reviewed and the business has explicitly approved that operating model.

## Manufacturer-first source policy

For technical facts about a manufacturer's own products, source priority is:

1. Official manufacturer product pages, datasheets, manuals, catalogs, configuration tools and technical support documentation.
2. Authorized distributor documentation, when manufacturer documentation is unavailable or when it provides territory-specific availability/pricing.
3. Reputable independent technical publications, certification records, standards bodies and research institutions as corroborating or contextual sources.
4. User reports, forums and reviews as experiential context, never as a substitute for authoritative specifications.

A manufacturer source is not downgraded merely because it is commercial. Its authority is assessed for the subject it controls: the manufacturer is normally the primary authority for its own product specifications. Marketing claims about superiority, competitor comparisons, performance in uncontrolled conditions or customer outcomes require separate qualification.

Keep these concepts separate in data:
- **Source authority** — who published or controls the source.
- **Source review status** — whether the exact page/document has been inspected.
- **Claim verification status** — whether a specific value is supported for the exact model/configuration.
- **Rights and reuse permission** — whether ROBOSCOPIA may reproduce, process, host or redistribute the material.
- **Commercial relationship** — whether a sponsorship, referral, affiliate or distribution agreement exists.

Public accessibility does not itself grant copyright, database, image, video or document reuse rights.

## Product and partnership data to plan for

As the architecture evolves, consider structured records for:
- Manufacturer identity and official domains.
- Product family, model, generation and configuration/variant.
- Official product URL and official document references, including revision/date.
- Authorized distributor/service partner, territory and validity period.
- Offer source, currency, observed price and observation date.
- Availability status and last-confirmed date.
- Enquiry/referral destination and attribution identifier.
- Commercial relationship type, agreement status, territory, covered products and expiry.
- Sponsorship/affiliate disclosure status.
- Warranty, installation, training and after-sales contact responsibilities where verified.

Do not invent missing prices, stock, lead times, distributor status or commercial terms. Time-sensitive offers must be dated and rechecked.

## Governance and safeguards

- No paid service, API, infrastructure, advertising spend or subscription without the owner's explicit approval.
- No outreach that commits ROBOSCOPIA to contractual terms without approval.
- No false claims of partnership, authorization, certification or distribution rights.
- No preferential technical ranking in exchange for payment.
- Maintain a visible correction/contact path for manufacturers.
- Keep audit trails for source changes, manufacturer submissions and commercial disclosures.
- Review applicable privacy, consumer, advertising, competition, tax, import/export and product-liability requirements before activating transactions or regional distribution.

## Success measures

Measure the platform's readiness and usefulness before optimizing revenue:
- Number and completeness of manufacturer and product profiles.
- Share of technical claims linked to reviewed primary sources.
- Coverage of variants and configuration-specific specifications.
- Manufacturer corrections handled and time to update.
- Qualified buyer enquiries and their conversion to quotations/demonstrations.
- Verified authorized distributor coverage by territory.
- User trust, comparison usefulness and correction rates.

## Implementation order

1. Preserve manufacturer-first source authority and source/claim/rights distinctions in the data model.
2. Improve product profiles, technical comparison and manufacturer attribution.
3. Build a permission-aware manufacturer correction/submission workflow.
4. Add buyer enquiry/RFQ and referral tracking only after privacy/security review.
5. Pilot transparent referral agreements.
6. Consider formal authorized distribution only when the audience, manufacturer demand, legal capacity and after-sales operations justify it.

**Current state:** This document records the approved strategic direction. It does not mean that commercial enquiry, affiliate, sales, payment or distribution features are currently implemented or that ROBOSCOPIA has any manufacturer agreement.
